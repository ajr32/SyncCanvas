# SyncCanvas Student Guide

SyncCanvas (also called CeeEss) helps you see what is happening at school and what you need to do next.

## Open your planner
Open the SyncCanvas home page on your family's computer or tablet. Your **Today** view shows your classes and school-day information. Your **Tonight** or homework area helps you keep track of work.

## Check your school day
Look at the class cards in order. A class with a scheduled period appears with its period information. On a day when school is closed, the planner may show a **No school today** message instead. A shortened day may show different class times.

## Keep track of your work
Choose the class that your task belongs to and add a short description. If a due date is available, check it before planning your work. You can mark work done when you finish. Your parent may review or confirm completed work.

## Lunch Buddies
If your parent has set up a Lunch Buddies activity, it appears on the days it meets. You may also see a note or task connected to that activity. Check the task and mark it done when finished.

## Saving and waiting
After pressing a save or submit button, wait for the page to finish responding before trying again. If an error appears, read the message and ask your parent for help rather than repeatedly pressing the button.

## If something looks wrong
If a class, time, assignment, or school-day message seems incorrect, tell your parent. SyncCanvas is a developing family planner; school information may be incomplete or need review.
