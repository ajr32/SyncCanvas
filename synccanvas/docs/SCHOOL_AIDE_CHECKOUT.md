# School aide / family check-out guide

This is a fast end-of-day review, not a replacement for teachers' instructions.

1. Open `/review`. Work through **every active class**, even classes with no entries.
2. Compare short student notes with read-only Canvas context. Ask whether any work is unfinished or needs to go home.
3. For each class save **Reviewed — work checked**, **Reviewed — nothing needed**, or **Reviewed — needs follow-up**.
4. Use **+ Add missing information** to capture verbal homework or unfinished work.
5. For uncertainty, enter a specific question and leave it unresolved until answered. Use the **Resolved** checkbox only after clarification.
6. Check the accordion pocket at the front of the binder and look for loose backpack papers. Save both checks and optional note. No need to sort every worksheet.
7. Confirm the reviewed count matches the number of active classes.

## If staff are unavailable
A parent can do the review later. These are local records, **not authenticated staff signatures**. Do not represent parent review as school verification.

## Quick acceptance test
With three active classes, review two and verify `2 of 3`; finish the third and verify `3 of 3`. Reload and confirm records persist. Test an unresolved question and paper check separately.
