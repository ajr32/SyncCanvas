# Recurring expectations

In `/settings`, find **Recurring expectations**. Select an active class, enter a concrete instruction such as “Practice math for 20 minutes”, and choose **Every day** or **Monday–Friday**. Use **Pause** and **Resume** as needed.

Each eligible day gets its own task instance; finishing one day should not complete future days. Pausing should prevent future appearance without rewriting past completion history.

## Suggested test
Add one disposable daily expectation, inspect `/afternoon`, complete today's instance, and confirm its status remains separate from later dates. Pause and resume. For weekday rules, use isolated test data rather than changing the computer clock.

This is a local work planner, not a push-notification system. Keep recurring expectations limited and actionable.
